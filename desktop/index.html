About Us

We are passionate architects & urbanists who decided to give their clients their needs with the best qualities & solutions.

CDD is a creative design firm based in Cairo that specializes in creating unique and functional designs and spaces. Founded in 2019, although being indulged in the field way before that, by Arch. Menna Habib & Arch. Samaher Soliman. The firm’s focus is on developing a strong identity for each project through concept driven approach, customized according to the client’s needs and goals.
